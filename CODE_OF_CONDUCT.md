# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces professional,
welcoming, and focused on constructive collaboration.

Acceptable participation includes:

- Giving specific, respectful, and actionable feedback
- Assuming good intent while discussing technical disagreements
- Respecting different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and correcting them
- Protecting private, personal, and security-sensitive information

Unacceptable participation includes harassment, discrimination, threats,
personal attacks, deliberate disruption, unwanted sexual attention, publishing
private information, or other conduct that would be inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate this standard
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.
Decisions should consider context, severity, prior behavior, and the safety of
the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other project-managed spaces.
